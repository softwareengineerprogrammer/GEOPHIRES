# Example: Superhot Rock (SHR) 6: Pumped 200 deg.C EGS Well with the coupled inflow-wellbore production wellbore model
#
# The 200 deg.C / 36.7 deg.C/km cell of the CATF SHR LCOE analysis (catf-shr-base) run through the same production
# wellbore chain as the superhot cells (example_SHR-4, example_SHR-5): Gringarten reservoir, the superhot-wellbore
# Darcy inflow and bottom-to-surface wellbore march, and the Coupled Wellbore Power Cycle plant (Power Plant Type 10). At
# 200 deg.C and 5.12 km with a productivity index of 0.75 kg/s/bar the 60 kg/s per well cannot reach the surface on
# its own, so the superhot-wellbore production pump stage (Coupled Wellbore Pump Policy, auto) sets a downhole pump at
# the shallowest depth with a liquid column below it (about 600 m, 190 deg.C intake) and lifts the flow to a liquid
# wellhead just above its vapor pressure. The production pumping power is subtracted from the plant output and the
# pumps are costed with the standard GEOPHIRES production pump correlation. Under the wellhead-state plant policy the
# liquid wellhead (about 191 deg.C) is served by GEOPHIRES's supercritical ORC correlation (correlation_orc plant
# path), so the plant is costed as a supercritical ORC plant.
#
# Requires the superhot-wellbore package:
#   pip install git+https://github.com/softwareengineerprogrammer/superhot-wellbore.git@geophires-client
